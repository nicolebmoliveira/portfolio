01 • BUSINESS CHALLENGE

Turn operational records into management-ready insights.

Consolidate sales, expenses, product usage, and package duration into a traceable view of profitability, cash flow, and operating capacity.

02 • DATA PREPARATION & CONTROLS

Build a reliable transaction-level foundation.

Standardize accounting groups, create unique sale and customer IDs, classify operating, investing, and financing cash flows, and model a clearly labeled synthetic AR aging schedule.

03 • FINANCIAL MODEL

Translate services into cost, margin, and profit per hour.

Allocate fixed costs, owner compensation, shared supplies, and service-specific products to calculate package economics and test pricing and utilization assumptions.

04 • KEY FINDINGS

Growth accelerated while the service mix remained concentrated.

Jan–Sep 2026 revenue increased 88.2% year over year. MD packages generated 82.3% of revenue, while larger packages delivered lower margins per hour because discounts scaled faster than costs.

05 • BUSINESS RECOMMENDATIONS

Improve package economics without abandoning the core offer.

Tighten large-package discounts, cross-sell high-margin services, track discount reasons, and concentrate promotions in months with spare capacity instead of peak months.

06 • DASHBOARD ROADMAP

Turn the model into a decision-ready Power BI experience.

Executive performance, service profitability, customer retention, expense and cash-flow analysis, AR aging, and theoretical versus billable-capacity utilization.
